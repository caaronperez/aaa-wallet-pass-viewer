Any size works. Pick a photo, or drop it on the card.

2Move and zoom the photo

Do this for the iPhone card and the Android pass: switch between them above the preview. Each one keeps its own position and zoom. Drag the photo to show the part you want, zoom in to show less of it. The photo always fills the whole space.

On a trackpad you can also pinch on the preview to zoom. With the keyboard: click the preview, then use the arrow keys to move and + / − to zoom.

3White veil (optional, iPhone only)

If the text at the bottom of the iPhone card is hard to read, turn on the white veil. It lightens only the bottom of the card behind the text. The Android pass doesn’t get a veil.

Pick the card and the club. You get one .zip with the folder ready to upload to AEM: the iPhone files in ios/ (artwork@2x.png 716 × 896 and artwork@3x.png 1074 × 1344) and the Android files in android/ (Hero.png 1032 × 812 and Logo.png), exactly as the previews show them.
